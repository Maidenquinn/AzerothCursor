# 🎯 AZEROTH CURSOR — 1.0.0
**Lost your cursor in a sea of spell effects? Find it again.**

Azeroth Cursor is a standalone addon for **Azeroth Survivors** that helps keep your mouse visible during intense runs!

✨ **MAKE IT YOURS**
• High-contrast ring with a black outline.
• Adjustable size, thickness and opacity.
• Optional glowing halo, halo animation, crosshair and center dot.
• **Balanced**, **Intense combat** and **Minimal** presets, with a live preview.

🎨 **EASY CUSTOMIZATION**
• Sliders plus +/− buttons for precise adjustments.
• Color wheel, brightness control and RGB sliders.
• Changing a setting automatically enables the marker so you can see the result immediately.
• Cancel a color change to restore your previous color and activation state.

🖱️ **YOUR CURSOR, YOUR CHOICE**
Your original cursor stays unchanged when you first install the addon. Enable the effects when you want them, or choose **Original** to switch them off. The marker lets all clicks pass through.

⚙️ Open settings with the **cyan minimap button** or `/azcursor`. Drag the minimap button to reposition it. French and English settings are included.

🔎 Can't find your mouse? Use **Locate cursor** or `/azcursor find` for a bright beacon!
